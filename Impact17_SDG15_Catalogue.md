## Action 1 — Plant a native tree

- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a person with a planted sapling/tree)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Choose a native species — Ghaf (the UAE's national tree), Sidr, or Date Palm. They survive heat and need little water.
- Get a sapling: your garden, school grounds with permission, or request a free Ghaf through Goumbook's Give a Ghaf programme.
- Plant in cooler months (October–March) for the best survival rate. Dig a hole twice the width of the root ball.
- Backfill with soil, water deeply, and add mulch to hold moisture.
- Photograph yourself with your newly planted tree.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What species did you plant, and why there?
- What surprised you about the process?
- What did you enjoy most?

## Action 2 — Set up a home composting bin

- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a compost bin/setup with organic waste)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Get a container — a purpose-made compost bin, or repurpose a large bucket with a lid and drilled air holes.
- Layer "greens" (fruit/vegetable scraps, coffee grounds) with "browns" (dry leaves, shredded cardboard, date palm fronds).
- Keep it in shade — UAE heat dries compost out fast. Add water occasionally to keep it damp, not wet.
- Turn the mix weekly with a stick or small fork.
- Photograph your composting setup.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What kitchen waste are you now diverting from the bin?
- How will you use the finished compost?
- What did you enjoy about starting this?

## Action 3 — Join or organise a desert or park clean-up

- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a person with collected litter/waste bags in an outdoor setting)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Pick a spot that needs it — a desert area, a wadi, a public park, or a community garden.
- Bring gloves, reusable bags, and water. Go early morning or late afternoon to avoid the heat.
- Sort what you collect into recyclables and general waste where possible.
- Check for organised clean-ups via Emirates Environmental Group or your community group — joining one counts.
- Photograph yourself with the litter you collected.

**Reflection prompts:**
- Where did you go, and what was the most common type of litter?
- Did you do this alone or with others?
- What did you enjoy about it?

## Action 4 — Build or install a bird/wildlife feeder or water station

- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a feeder or water station, ideally placed outdoors)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- In UAE summers, water matters more than food — set out a shallow dish of clean water in shade for birds.
- For a feeder, repurpose a plastic bottle or wooden box; place it where cats can't reach.
- Position it where you can see it but where animals feel safe — a balcony, garden, or courtyard.
- Refresh the water daily; it evaporates and gets dirty fast in the heat.
- Photograph your installed feeder or water station.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What wildlife have you seen visit, or do you hope to see?
- Where did you place it and why?
- What did you enjoy about making it?

## Action 5 — Grow a pollinator-friendly plant

- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a potted or planted flowering plant)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Choose something that handles UAE heat and attracts bees and butterflies — basil, mint, lavender, or native wildflowers.
- A pot on a balcony works fine — you don't need a garden.
- Use well-draining soil and water in the early morning or evening.
- Keep it where it gets sun but some afternoon shade in peak summer.
- Photograph your plant.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What did you choose to grow, and why?
- Have you seen any insects visit it yet?
- What did you enjoy about this?

## Action 6 — Remove an invasive plant or replace it with a native one

- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for plant removal/replacement activity)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Learn one common invasive in the UAE — for example, Prosopis juliflora (mesquite), often confused with the native Ghaf.
- With permission, remove a small invasive plant from your garden or community area, roots included.
- Replace it with a native: Ghaf, Sidr, or a desert shrub.
- Dispose of the invasive properly so it doesn't reseed.
- Photograph the removal or the newly planted native.

**Reflection prompts:**
- Which plant did you remove or replace?
- What did you learn about why invasives are a problem?
- What did you enjoy about it?

## Action 7 — Create a small native plant patch or "rewild" a corner

- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a planted area with multiple plants)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Pick a neglected corner — a strip of garden, a cluster of large pots, a school plot.
- Plant a mix of UAE natives: Ghaf, Sidr, desert grasses, native shrubs.
- Group plants with similar water needs together to save water.
- Leave it a little wild — fallen leaves and seed heads support insects and birds.
- Photograph your native patch.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What did you plant in your patch?
- How is it different from a regular garden bed?
- What did you enjoy about creating it?

## Action 8 — Make a poster or infographic about a UAE endangered species

- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for a hand-made or designed poster/infographic on the topic)
- **Primary SDG:** 15
- *(Rewritten from the original non-verifiable "raise awareness about biodiversity")*

**How to do this:**
- Choose a UAE species under threat — Arabian oryx, Arabian leopard, sand cat, spiny-tailed lizard (dhub).
- Find three to five key facts: where it lives, why it's threatened, what helps it.
- Make a poster by hand or design one digitally — clear, simple, visual.
- Display it where people will see it, or share it.
- Photograph your finished poster.

**Reflection prompts:**

- Where did you display or share your poster?

## Action 9 — Start a windowsill or balcony herb garden

- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for potted herbs)
- **Primary SDG:** 15

**How to do this:**
- Pick herbs that grow well in the UAE — mint, basil, coriander, parsley.
- Use small pots with drainage holes and good potting soil.
- Place on a windowsill or balcony with morning sun and afternoon shade.
- Water regularly — pots dry out fast here — and harvest leaves as they grow.
- Photograph your herb garden.

**Reflection prompts:**
- What herbs did you plant?
- How will you use them?
- What did you enjoy about starting this?

## Action 10 — Visit a UAE nature reserve or protected area and document it

- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10
- **Verification:** Photo (AI checks for an outdoor nature/reserve setting with the user present)
- **Primary SDG:** 15
- *(Rewritten from the original non-verifiable "appreciate nature")*

**How to do this:**
- Choose a protected area — Jubail Mangrove Park, Al Wathba Wetland Reserve, Wadi Wurayah, or Sir Bani Yas Island.
- Check opening times and any booking requirements before you go.
- While there, observe the plants and animals; note what's specific to that ecosystem.
- Stay on marked paths and take only photos.
- Photograph yourself or the landscape at the reserve.

**Reflection prompts:**
- Where did you go, and what ecosystem did you see?
- What plant or animal stood out to you?
- What did you enjoy about the visit?
