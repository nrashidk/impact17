# SDG 4 — Quality Education

## 4.1 — Tutor or mentor someone in a subject you know
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of a tutoring session/materials
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a subject you're strong in and someone who'd benefit — a younger student, a classmate, a sibling.
  - Plan a few short, focused sessions.
  - Be patient and encouraging; check they understand.
  - Keep it consistent.
  - Photograph a session or the materials you prepared (no faces needed).
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you teach, and to whom? What was challenging? What did you enjoy?

## 4.2 — Create a learning resource and share it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the resource
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a topic you understand well.
  - Make a clear resource — a study sheet, flashcards, a short explainer.
  - Make it genuinely useful for someone learning it fresh.
  - Share it with classmates or online.
  - Photograph your resource.
- **Reflection prompts:** What topic did you cover? Who did you share it with? What did you enjoy?

## 4.3 — Donate books to a school, library, or community shelf
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the books at/near the donation point
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather books in good condition you no longer need.
  - Find a recipient — a community library, a school, a little free library, a charity.
  - Sort them by age suitability.
  - Drop them off.
  - Photograph the books at the donation point.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you donate? Who will use them? What did you enjoy?

## 4.4 — Read a book outside your usual subjects and summarise it
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written summary *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a book on a topic you don't usually explore.
  - Read it fully.
  - Write a short summary of the main ideas and what you learned.
  - Keep your notes.
  - Photograph your written summary.
- **Reflection prompts:** Why this book? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?

## 4.5 — Set up a study group and run a session
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the study group/materials
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather a few classmates working on the same subject.
  - Pick a topic and a time.
  - Structure the session so everyone contributes.
  - Run it.
  - Photograph the study session or its materials.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you study? Did the group help? What did you enjoy?

## 4.6 — Teach a practical skill to someone
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the skill-teaching activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a practical skill you have — a craft, a tech skill, cooking, a sport.
  - Find someone who wants to learn it.
  - Break it into simple steps and guide them through.
  - Let them try it themselves.
  - Photograph the activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** What skill did you teach? How did it go? What did you enjoy?

## 4.7 — Make an educational poster or explainer on a topic you care about
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a topic you think more people should understand.
  - Research it properly.
  - Design a clear, accurate poster or explainer.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Why this topic? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 4.8 — Organise a book or stationery swap
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the swap event/setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a swap where people bring books or supplies to exchange.
  - Get permission for a space at school or in your community.
  - Spread the word and set simple rules.
  - Run the event; donate leftovers.
  - Photograph the swap setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you organise? How did it go? What did you enjoy?

## 4.9 — Learn something new online and document your progress
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of notes/project from the learning *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a skill or topic and a free, reputable course or tutorial.
  - Work through it properly over several sessions.
  - Take notes or build something with what you learned.
  - Track your progress.
  - Photograph your notes or project.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you learn? Will you keep going? What did you enjoy?

## 4.10 — Help improve a learning space
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the improved space
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a learning space that could be better — a classroom corner, a home study area, a library shelf.
  - Plan a practical improvement — organising, labelling, decorating, adding resources.
  - Get permission if it's shared.
  - Make the improvement.
  - Photograph the improved space.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you improve? Who benefits? What did you enjoy?
