# SDG 1 — No Poverty

*Note: SDG 1 has few naturally photographable individual actions. Most actions here are the "create an artefact / do a documented activity" pattern.*

## 1.1 — Assemble and donate a care package
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of assembled package
- **How to do this:**
  - Put together essentials: non-perishable food, hygiene items, or clean clothes in good condition.
  - Find a recipient channel — Emirates Red Crescent, a local mosque collection, or a community drive.
  - Pack items clearly and respectfully.
  - Deliver or drop off at the collection point.
  - Photograph your assembled care package before donating.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you include and why? Where did it go? What did you enjoy?

## 1.2 — Donate usable items to a charity collection
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of items at/near a donation point
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather clothes, books, toys, or household items in good condition.
  - Locate a UAE donation point — Emirates Red Crescent bins, Dubai Charity Association, or a school drive.
  - Clean and sort the items before donating.
  - Take them to the collection point.
  - Photograph your items at the donation point.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you donate? Why those items? What did you enjoy?

## 1.3 — Volunteer at a food bank or community kitchen
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the user volunteering
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a UAE food distribution effort — UAE Food Bank, Emirates Red Crescent, or a Ramadan iftar drive.
  - Register for a volunteer slot in advance.
  - Show up on time, follow the team's instructions, and commit for the full session.
  - Respect the dignity and privacy of everyone you serve.
  - Photograph yourself volunteering (with permission, no recipients identifiable).
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you do during your session? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?

## 1.4 — Make an informational poster about a UAE social support service
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research one real UAE support service — UAE Food Bank, Community Development Authority programmes, or Zakat Fund.
  - Note who it helps and how someone accesses it.
  - Design a clear poster — by hand or digitally.
  - Display or share it where people who need it might see it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which service did you choose? What surprised you? What did you enjoy?

## 1.5 — Organise a small fundraising activity
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the fundraising activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a registered UAE cause and a simple format — a bake sale, a sponsored walk, a class collection.
  - Get any needed permission from your school, building, or community.
  - Run the activity and keep the proceeds transparent.
  - Donate the total to the chosen cause through official channels.
  - Photograph your fundraising activity in progress.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you organise and for whom? What was challenging? What did you enjoy?

## 1.6 — Cook and share a meal with someone in need
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the prepared meal
- **How to do this:**
  - Prepare a simple, generous meal.
  - Identify a respectful way to share it — a labourer's accommodation drive, a community iftar, a neighbour facing hardship.
  - Package it cleanly and safely for transport.
  - Deliver it personally or through an organised channel.
  - Photograph the prepared meal before sharing.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you cook? How did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 1.7 — Write and share your own "budgeting basics" guide
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Write a one-page guide on managing money — saving a little regularly, needs vs wants, avoiding debt.
  - Keep it simple and practical for someone your age.
  - Make it visual and easy to read.
  - Share it with classmates, family, or online.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's the most useful tip in your guide? Who did you share it with? What did you enjoy?

## 1.8 — Collect and donate school supplies
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of collected supplies
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather new or gently used school supplies — notebooks, pens, bags, calculators.
  - Run a small collection among family or classmates.
  - Sort and pack the supplies neatly.
  - Donate through a school programme or charity that supports students in need.
  - Photograph the collected supplies.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you collect? Who will it help? What did you enjoy?

## 1.9 — Create a "give back" jar and document filling it
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the jar *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up a jar or container for spare change or a small regular contribution.
  - Label it with the cause you're saving for.
  - Add to it consistently over time.
  - When it's full, donate the total to a registered UAE charity.
  - Photograph your jar.
- **Reflection prompts:** What cause did you choose? How long did it take to fill? What did you enjoy?

## 1.10 — Interview a family or community member about overcoming hardship
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of written interview notes/summary *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Ask someone older in your family or community if they'll share their story.
  - Prepare a few respectful questions about challenges and resilience.
  - Listen carefully and take notes.
  - Write a short summary of what you learned.
  - Photograph your written notes or summary.
- **Reflection prompts:** What stayed with you from their story? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?
