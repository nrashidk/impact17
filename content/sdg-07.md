# SDG 7 — Affordable and Clean Energy

## 7.1 — Switch your home to energy-efficient lighting
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of installed LED bulbs
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify older, less efficient bulbs in your home.
  - Replace them with LED bulbs — they use far less power and handle UAE heat better.
  - Recycle the old bulbs properly rather than binning them.
  - Note how many you switched.
  - Photograph your installed LED bulbs.
- **Reflection prompts:** How many bulbs did you switch? What will it save? What did you enjoy?

## 7.2 — Track and reduce your household electricity use
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the written tracking log *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Check your household's electricity use from the DEWA/ADDC bill or meter.
  - Make specific changes — AC at 24°C, unplugging idle devices, using daylight.
  - Track use over two to three weeks.
  - Record the reduction.
  - Photograph your tracking log.
- **Reflection prompts:** What change saved the most? How much did you cut? What did you enjoy?

## 7.3 — Build a small solar-powered device
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the working solar device
- **How to do this:**
  - Get a small solar panel and a simple project — a solar phone charger, a solar light, a small fan.
  - Follow a build guide and assemble it.
  - Test it in the sun — the UAE has no shortage of that.
  - Make sure it actually works.
  - Photograph your working solar device.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you build? Did it work first time? What did you enjoy?

## 7.4 — Set up a "switch-off" routine at home or school
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the routine/checklist *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Make a checklist of things to switch off when leaving a room or building — lights, screens, AC.
  - Put it where people will see it.
  - Get your household or class to follow it.
  - Run it for at least a week.
  - Photograph your checklist in place.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's on your checklist? Did people follow it? What did you enjoy?

## 7.5 — Make a poster on the UAE's clean energy projects
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research UAE clean energy — Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum Solar Park, Barakah, Masdar City.
  - Pick a few clear facts about scale and impact.
  - Design a poster by hand or digitally.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which project impressed you most? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 7.6 — Do an energy audit of your home or classroom
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written audit *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Walk through the space and note every energy use — lighting, AC, appliances, devices.
  - Identify waste and where efficiency is possible.
  - Write up findings with recommendations.
  - Share them with your family or school.
  - Photograph your written audit.
- **Reflection prompts:** What was the biggest waste? What did you recommend? What did you enjoy?

## 7.7 — Use natural light and ventilation for a day, documented
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the daylit space *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a cooler day. Use daylight instead of electric lighting where you can.
  - Open windows for ventilation where the temperature allows.
  - Notice what's genuinely practical in the UAE climate and what isn't.
  - Reflect on the limits and the wins.
  - Photograph your naturally lit space.
- **Reflection prompts:** What worked? What didn't, given the climate? What did you enjoy?

## 7.8 — Help install or maintain solar lights outdoors
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the solar lights
- **How to do this:**
  - Get solar garden or pathway lights, or maintain existing ones.
  - Place them where they get full sun during the day.
  - Make sure they charge and switch on properly.
  - Use them to replace mains-powered outdoor lighting.
  - Photograph the installed solar lights.
- **Reflection prompts:** Where did you install them? What do they replace? What did you enjoy?

## 7.9 — Create an energy-saving guide for your household
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - List practical, UAE-specific energy habits — AC settings, peak-hour awareness, appliance use.
  - Write and design a clear one-page guide.
  - Put it somewhere visible.
  - Talk your household through it.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which tip matters most for your home? Did your family adopt it? What did you enjoy?

## 7.10 — Organise an energy awareness display or session
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the display/session
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose an angle — clean energy, efficiency, the UAE's energy transition.
  - Research accurate information.
  - Build a display or prepare a session for classmates or community.
  - Set it up where people will engage with it.
  - Photograph your display or session.
- **Reflection prompts:** What angle did you choose? How did people respond? What did you enjoy?
