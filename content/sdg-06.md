# SDG 6 — Clean Water and Sanitation

## 6.1 — Fix a leaking tap or report leaks
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the repair or reported leak
- **How to do this:**
  - Check taps and pipes at home or school for drips and leaks.
  - Fix a simple leak yourself (a worn washer) or report it to whoever maintains the building.
  - Note how much water a single drip wastes over a day.
  - Confirm the fix.
  - Photograph the repaired tap or the leak you reported.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you find? How much water does it save? What did you enjoy?

## 6.2 — Install a water-saving device
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the installed device
- **How to do this:**
  - Get a simple water-saver — a tap aerator, a low-flow showerhead, or a cistern displacement bag.
  - Install it following the instructions.
  - Many UAE homes already have these — if so, install one somewhere that doesn't, or help a neighbour.
  - Check it works.
  - Photograph the installed device.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you install, and where? What difference will it make? What did you enjoy?

## 6.3 — Track and reduce your household water use
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the written tracking log *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Note your household's water use from the bill or meter.
  - Make specific changes — shorter showers, full loads, no running taps.
  - Track use over two to three weeks.
  - Record the reduction.
  - Photograph your tracking log.
- **Reflection prompts:** What changes worked best? How much did you save? What did you enjoy?

## 6.4 — Set up a greywater reuse system for plants
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the greywater setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Collect safe greywater — water from rinsing vegetables, or cooled water from washing.
  - Store it in a clean container.
  - Use it for non-edible plants rather than fresh tap water.
  - Set up a simple routine.
  - Photograph your greywater reuse setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** Where does your greywater come from? What does it water? What did you enjoy?

## 6.5 — Make a poster on water scarcity in the UAE
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research why water is scarce in the UAE — desalination, low rainfall, high per-person use.
  - Pick three to five clear facts.
  - Design a poster by hand or digitally.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which fact surprised you? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 6.6 — Organise a clean-up of a water area
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the clean-up
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a water area — a beach, a creek, a wadi, a corniche.
  - Bring gloves, bags, and water; go at a cooler time.
  - Collect and sort litter, especially plastic.
  - Join an organised clean-up if there's one running.
  - Photograph yourself with the collected litter.
- **Reflection prompts:** Where did you go? What did you find most of? What did you enjoy?

## 6.7 — Collect condensation or AC water for reuse
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the collection setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Locate where your AC unit's condensate drains — it produces usable water, especially in summer.
  - Place a clean container to collect it safely.
  - Use the collected water for non-edible plants or cleaning.
  - Empty and maintain it regularly.
  - Photograph your collection setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** How much water does it collect? What do you use it for? What did you enjoy?

## 6.8 — Do a home or school water audit
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written audit *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Walk through your home or school and check every water point — taps, toilets, hoses, irrigation.
  - Note leaks, waste, and where savings are possible.
  - Write up your findings with recommendations.
  - Share them with your family or school.
  - Photograph your written audit.
- **Reflection prompts:** What was the biggest waste you found? What did you recommend? What did you enjoy?

## 6.9 — Create a water-saving guide for your household
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - List practical, specific water-saving habits for daily life.
  - Write and design a clear one-page guide.
  - Put it somewhere visible at home.
  - Talk your household through it.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which tip is most useful for your home? Did your family adopt it? What did you enjoy?

## 6.10 — Set up rainwater or condensation harvesting for a garden
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the harvesting setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up containers to catch the UAE's occasional rain, or scale up condensation collection.
  - Position them to catch runoff — under a drainpipe or sloped surface.
  - Cover them to keep them clean and reduce evaporation.
  - Use the water for the garden.
  - Photograph your harvesting setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** How does your system work? What does it water? What did you enjoy?
