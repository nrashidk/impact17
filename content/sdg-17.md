# SDG 17 — Partnerships for the Goals

*Note: SDG 17 is a soft, meta-goal about collaboration. Actions here focus on documented collective action and partnership-building.*

## 17.1 — Organise a group action on another SDG
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the group action
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick any other SDG and an action from it.
  - Instead of doing it alone, organise a group to do it together.
  - Coordinate the people, time, and task.
  - Carry it out as a team.
  - Photograph the group action.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did your group achieve together? Why did teamwork help? What did you enjoy?

## 17.2 — Partner with a local organisation on a project
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the partnership activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a local group, charity, or business working on something you care about.
  - Propose a small joint project or offer real help.
  - Agree on what each side contributes.
  - Carry out the work together.
  - Photograph the partnership activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who did you partner with? What did the partnership add? What did you enjoy?

## 17.3 — Connect two people or groups who should collaborate
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written record of the connection *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Notice two people or groups who'd achieve more together.
  - Introduce them and explain why collaboration would help.
  - Support them in starting a conversation.
  - Note what came of it.
  - Photograph your written record of the connection.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who did you connect, and why? What happened? What did you enjoy?

## 17.4 — Create a resource-sharing system
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the sharing system
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up a way for a group to share resources — skills, tools, materials, knowledge.
  - Make it organised and fair to use.
  - Launch it with a real group.
  - Make sure it's actually used.
  - Photograph your sharing system.
- **Reflection prompts:** What does your system share? Did people use it? What did you enjoy?

## 17.5 — Make a map of who's working on the SDGs in the UAE
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the map *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research UAE organisations, initiatives, and programmes working on the SDGs.
  - Organise them into a clear map by goal or theme.
  - Make it useful for someone wanting to get involved.
  - Share it.
  - Photograph your finished map.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who's doing the most interesting work? What did you enjoy?

## 17.6 — Run a collaborative fundraising or awareness drive
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the drive
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a cause and gather multiple people or groups to run a drive together.
  - Divide roles fairly.
  - Run the drive as a genuine collaboration.
  - Channel the result to the cause properly.
  - Photograph the drive in action.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who collaborated? What did working together make possible? What did you enjoy?

## 17.7 — Document and share a successful partnership
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written case study *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a partnership that worked — local or well-known.
  - Research what made it succeed.
  - Write it up as a short case study with lessons.
  - Share it with people who could learn from it.
  - Photograph your written case study.
- **Reflection prompts:** What made the partnership work? What did you enjoy?

## 17.8 — Teach a skill to enable someone else's SDG action
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the teaching activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a skill someone needs to take their own sustainability action.
  - Teach it to them properly.
  - Make sure they can now act on their own.
  - Note what your teaching enabled.
  - Photograph the teaching activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** What skill did you pass on? What will they do with it? What did you enjoy?

## 17.9 — Build a cross-community collaboration
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the collaboration
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify two communities or groups that don't usually work together — different schools, neighbourhoods, age groups.
  - Find a shared goal.
  - Bring them together on it.
  - Carry out a joint activity.
  - Photograph the collaboration.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which communities did you bring together? What did they achieve? What did you enjoy?

## 17.10 — Create a pledge or commitment campaign
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the campaign/pledges
- **How to do this:**
  - Design a campaign where people publicly commit to a specific SDG action.
  - Make the pledge concrete and trackable.
  - Gather real commitments from a group.
  - Set up a way to follow through.
  - Photograph your campaign or the collected pledges.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did people pledge? How will you track it? What did you enjoy?
