# SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities

## 11.1 — Use public or shared transport for a week and log it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the transport log/tickets *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - For a week, choose metro, bus, carpooling, cycling, or walking over a private car where possible.
  - Track each trip.
  - Note what worked and what was difficult.
  - Total the car trips you avoided.
  - Photograph your transport log or tickets.
- **Reflection prompts:** What was easiest? What was hard? What did you enjoy?

## 11.2 — Improve a shared community space
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the improved space
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a neglected shared space — a corridor, a courtyard, a garden, a bus stop area.
  - Plan a practical improvement and get permission.
  - Do the work — cleaning, planting, organising, repairing.
  - Make sure it lasts.
  - Photograph the improved space.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you improve? Who benefits? What did you enjoy?

## 11.3 — Map safe walking or cycling routes in your area
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the route map *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Walk or cycle your neighbourhood and note safe, shaded, well-connected routes.
  - Mark hazards and gaps too.
  - Create a clear map.
  - Share it with neighbours or classmates.
  - Photograph your route map.
- **Reflection prompts:** What makes a route good in the UAE climate? What did you enjoy?

## 11.4 — Organise a neighbourhood clean-up
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the clean-up
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick an area that needs it.
  - Invite neighbours or classmates; pick a cooler time.
  - Bring gloves, bags, water; sort recyclables.
  - Run the clean-up.
  - Photograph the clean-up in progress.
- **Reflection prompts:** Where did you clean? Who joined? What did you enjoy?

## 11.5 — Document and report a public hazard or maintenance issue
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the issue reported *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Spot a real public issue — a broken light, a damaged path, an overflowing bin.
  - Photograph and locate it.
  - Report it through the right channel — your municipality's app or service line.
  - Keep a record of the report.
  - Photograph the issue you reported.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you report? Why does it matter? What did you enjoy?

## 11.6 — Add greenery to an urban space
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the planted greenery
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a hard, grey space that could take some green — with permission.
  - Choose climate-suitable, low-water plants.
  - Plant them and set up watering.
  - Make sure it's maintainable.
  - Photograph the greenery you added.
- **Reflection prompts:** Where did you add greenery? What did you plant? What did you enjoy?

## 11.7 — Create a guide to your community's local services
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Map the useful services near you — clinics, libraries, recycling points, parks, transport.
  - Lay them out in a clear, simple guide.
  - Make it genuinely useful for a newcomer.
  - Share it with neighbours.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** What service is most overlooked? Who did you share it with? What did you enjoy?

## 11.8 — Design a improvement for your neighbourhood
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the design/proposal *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify something that would make your neighbourhood better — shade, seating, a crossing, a green corner.
  - Research why it's needed.
  - Design it — drawings, a model, a proposal.
  - Share it with neighbours or local authorities.
  - Photograph your design.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you propose? Is it realistic? What did you enjoy?

## 11.9 — Set up a community sharing point
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the sharing point
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up a small shelf or box for sharing — books, tools, plant cuttings.
  - Find a suitable, permitted spot.
  - Stock it and label how it works.
  - Tell neighbours about it.
  - Photograph your sharing point.
- **Reflection prompts:** What does your sharing point offer? Did people use it? What did you enjoy?

## 11.10 — Photograph and present your community's heritage
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the heritage feature/presentation
- **How to do this:**
  - Find heritage near you — old architecture, a souk, a cultural site, traditions.
  - Document it thoughtfully.
  - Put together a short presentation or display about why it matters.
  - Share it.
  - Photograph the heritage feature or your presentation.
- **Reflection prompts:** What heritage did you document? Why does it matter? What did you enjoy?
