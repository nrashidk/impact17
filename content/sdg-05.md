# SDG 5 — Gender Equality

*Note: SDG 5 has very few naturally photographable individual actions. Most actions here follow the "create an artefact / document an activity" pattern.*

## 5.1 — Make a poster celebrating women's contributions in the UAE
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research Emirati women who've shaped the country — in government, science, arts, sport, business.
  - Choose a few and note their achievements.
  - Design a clear, celebratory poster.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Whose story stood out? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 5.2 — Interview a woman you admire and write up her story
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written interview/summary *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a woman in your family or community whose path you respect.
  - Prepare thoughtful, respectful questions.
  - Listen and take notes.
  - Write up her story.
  - Photograph your written summary.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you learn from her? What surprised you? What did you enjoy?

## 5.3 — Create a guide on respect and equality for younger students
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Think through simple, concrete ideas about treating everyone fairly and with respect.
  - Write and illustrate an age-appropriate guide for younger children.
  - Use clear examples.
  - Share it with a younger class or sibling.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** What ideas did you focus on? Who did you share it with? What did you enjoy?

## 5.4 — Organise an activity that includes everyone equally
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a game, sport, or project designed so everyone can take part equally.
  - Think about who usually gets left out and design against that.
  - Invite a mixed group.
  - Run the activity.
  - Photograph the activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** How did you make it inclusive? Did it work? What did you enjoy?

## 5.5 — Research and present on a woman leader in a field you like
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the presentation/materials *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a field that interests you — science, sport, art, business, politics.
  - Find a woman leader in that field and research her path.
  - Prepare a short presentation or display.
  - Present it to your class, family, or community.
  - Photograph your presentation materials.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who did you choose and why? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?

## 5.6 — Write an article or post on why gender equality matters
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo/screenshot of the written piece *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Reflect on why equal opportunity matters, with concrete examples.
  - Write a clear, honest short article or post.
  - Keep it thoughtful rather than slogan-based.
  - Share it — a school newsletter, a blog, social media.
  - Photograph or screenshot your published piece.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's your main point? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 5.7 — Document and share unpaid work in your household
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written log/reflection *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - For a few days, note all the unpaid household work done — by everyone.
  - Look at how it's shared.
  - Write a short reflection on what you noticed.
  - Talk with your family about sharing it more evenly.
  - Photograph your written log or reflection.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you notice about how work is shared? What changed? What did you enjoy?

## 5.8 — Take on a task usually not expected of your gender
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of doing the task *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a useful task that stereotypes might say isn't "for you."
  - Learn to do it properly.
  - Do it as a genuine contribution, not a one-off.
  - Reflect on any assumptions it challenged.
  - Photograph yourself doing the task.
- **Reflection prompts:** What task did you take on? Did it challenge any assumptions? What did you enjoy?

## 5.9 — Create artwork celebrating equality
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the artwork *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan an artwork — drawing, painting, digital, collage — on the theme of equality.
  - Put real thought into the message.
  - Complete it.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished artwork.
- **Reflection prompts:** What does your artwork say? Where did you show it? What did you enjoy?

## 5.10 — Organise a discussion or awareness session on equality
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the session
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a respectful discussion or awareness session for classmates or a community group.
  - Prepare a few prompts or facts to guide it.
  - Get permission for the space and time.
  - Facilitate it so everyone can speak.
  - Photograph the session.
- **Reflection prompts:** What came up in the discussion? What did people take away? What did you enjoy?
