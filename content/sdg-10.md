# SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities

*Note: SDG 10 is a soft SDG — few photographable individual actions. Mostly "create / document / organise" pattern.*

## 10.1 — Organise an activity that includes an often-excluded group
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Think about who gets left out in your school or community.
  - Design an activity built specifically to include them.
  - Invite them genuinely, not as a token.
  - Run it.
  - Photograph the activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** Who did you focus on including? Did it work? What did you enjoy?

## 10.2 — Learn basic sign language or accessibility skills
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of practice notes/materials *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose an accessibility skill — basic sign language, guiding, accessible communication.
  - Find a reputable resource and practise.
  - Learn enough to actually use it.
  - Keep your practice notes.
  - Photograph your notes or practice materials.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you learn? When could you use it? What did you enjoy?

## 10.3 — Make a poster celebrating the UAE's cultural diversity
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - The UAE is home to many nationalities — research that diversity.
  - Choose a respectful, celebratory angle.
  - Design a poster.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you want your poster to say? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 10.4 — Audit a space for accessibility
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written audit *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a building or public space.
  - Check it against accessibility needs — ramps, signage, seating, clear paths.
  - Note what works and what doesn't.
  - Write up findings with suggestions.
  - Photograph your written audit.
- **Reflection prompts:** What barriers did you find? What did you suggest? What did you enjoy?

## 10.5 — Interview someone with a different background to yours
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written summary *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Find someone whose background differs from yours — culture, generation, life path.
  - Ask respectful questions about their experiences and perspective.
  - Listen genuinely.
  - Write up what you learned.
  - Photograph your written summary.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you learn? What assumption did it challenge? What did you enjoy?

## 10.6 — Translate something useful for a non-native speaker
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the translated material *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Find useful information someone might struggle to access in their language.
  - Translate it accurately into a language you know.
  - Make it clear and easy to read.
  - Share it with someone who needs it.
  - Photograph the translated material.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you translate, and for whom? What did you enjoy?

## 10.7 — Create an artwork or piece on inclusion
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the work *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a creative piece — art, writing, video — on belonging and inclusion.
  - Put real thought into the message.
  - Complete it.
  - Share it.
  - Photograph your finished work.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's your message? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 10.8 — Help someone access a service or opportunity
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo documenting the help given *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Find someone facing a barrier — a form, an application, a process they can't easily navigate.
  - Help them through it, respectfully and without taking over.
  - Make sure they can do it themselves next time.
  - Note the barrier you helped remove.
  - Photograph the materials or setting (no identifying details).
- **Reflection prompts:** What barrier did you help with? What did you enjoy?

## 10.9 — Run an awareness session on a form of inequality
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the session
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a specific inequality issue you can address respectfully.
  - Research it accurately.
  - Prepare a session for classmates or a community group.
  - Facilitate it so people can engage and ask questions.
  - Photograph the session.
- **Reflection prompts:** What issue did you cover? What did people take away? What did you enjoy?

## 10.10 — Start an inclusive club or group
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the club/group activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a shared interest that could bring a mixed group together.
  - Set it up with inclusion as a core rule.
  - Get permission for space and time if needed.
  - Run the first session.
  - Photograph the club's activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's the club about? Who joined? What did you enjoy?
