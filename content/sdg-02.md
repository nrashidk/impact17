# SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

## 2.1 — Grow a vegetable from seed to harvest
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the growing/harvested vegetable
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose something that grows in the UAE climate — tomatoes, chillies, leafy greens, in cooler months.
  - Use a pot or garden bed with good soil and drainage.
  - Water consistently, morning or evening, and protect from peak afternoon sun.
  - Be patient — track it through to harvest.
  - Photograph your growing or harvested vegetable.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you grow? What was hardest? What did you enjoy?

## 2.2 — Donate non-perishable food to a UAE food drive
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of food items at/near a collection point
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather non-perishable items — rice, lentils, canned goods, dates — within date.
  - Find a collection point — UAE Food Bank, a mosque, or a supermarket drive.
  - Check expiry dates and pack securely.
  - Drop off at the collection point.
  - Photograph your donation at the collection point.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you donate? Why those items? What did you enjoy?

## 2.3 — Cook a meal using only leftovers or about-to-expire food
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the prepared meal
- **How to do this:**
  - Check your fridge for food near its use-by date or leftovers needing using up.
  - Plan a meal around what you find rather than buying new ingredients.
  - Cook it and serve it.
  - Note how much food you saved from being wasted.
  - Photograph your finished meal.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you make? What would have been wasted otherwise? What did you enjoy?

## 2.4 — Start a herb or vegetable patch at school or home
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the planted patch
- **How to do this:**
  - Find space — a school plot with permission, a garden corner, or a cluster of large pots.
  - Choose climate-suitable herbs and vegetables.
  - Prepare the soil, plant, and set up a watering routine.
  - Get others involved in maintaining it if it's at school.
  - Photograph your planted patch.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you plant? Who will help maintain it? What did you enjoy?

## 2.5 — Make a meal plan that reduces food waste
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written meal plan *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a week of meals for your household.
  - Build a shopping list that uses ingredients across multiple meals so nothing is left to spoil.
  - Note portion sizes to avoid over-cooking.
  - Write or print the plan.
  - Photograph your finished meal plan.
- **Reflection prompts:** How does your plan cut waste? Will you keep using it? What did you enjoy?

## 2.6 — Share a meal with neighbours or community
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the shared meal/setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Prepare extra of a dish you're already making.
  - Share it with neighbours, building staff, or a community gathering — an iftar is a natural fit.
  - Package it cleanly and safely.
  - Deliver it personally where appropriate.
  - Photograph the meal you prepared to share.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you share, and with whom? What did you enjoy?

## 2.7 — Learn to preserve food and document it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the preserved food *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a preservation method — drying, pickling, freezing, or making jam.
  - Choose produce that's in season or abundant.
  - Follow a safe method carefully.
  - Store the result properly and label it.
  - Photograph your preserved food.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you preserve and how? Why does this reduce waste? What did you enjoy?

## 2.8 — Make a poster about food waste in the UAE
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research food waste facts specific to the UAE — especially around Ramadan and large events.
  - Pick three to five clear, surprising points.
  - Design a poster by hand or digitally.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which fact surprised you most? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 2.9 — Set up a compost system for food scraps
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the compost setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Get a bin or container with air holes and a lid.
  - Add fruit and vegetable scraps; avoid meat and dairy.
  - Keep it shaded and slightly damp — UAE heat dries it fast.
  - Turn it weekly.
  - Photograph your compost setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** What scraps are you now composting? How will you use the result? What did you enjoy?

## 2.10 — Volunteer with a food distribution effort
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the user volunteering
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a UAE effort — UAE Food Bank, Emirates Red Crescent, or a Ramadan meal drive.
  - Register for a volunteer slot ahead of time.
  - Commit to the full session and follow the team's process.
  - Handle food hygienically and treat recipients with dignity.
  - Photograph yourself volunteering (no recipients identifiable).
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you do? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?
