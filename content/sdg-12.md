# SDG 12 — Responsible Consumption and Production

## 12.1 — Do a one-week waste audit
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written audit *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - For a week, track everything your household throws away.
  - Sort it by type — plastic, food, paper, packaging.
  - Identify your biggest waste streams.
  - Write up findings and a reduction plan.
  - Photograph your written audit.
- **Reflection prompts:** What was your biggest waste stream? What will you change? What did you enjoy?

## 12.2 — Recycle at a Sparklo machine or recycling point
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo at the recycling point
- **How to do this:**
  - Collect clean plastic bottles and aluminium cans.
  - Find a Sparklomat or recycling point — many UAE malls, schools, and community spots have them.
  - Deposit your items.
  - Note how many you recycled.
  - Photograph yourself at the recycling point.
- **Reflection prompts:** How many items did you recycle? Where? What did you enjoy?

## 12.3 — Switch to reusables and document the swap
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the reusable items *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify single-use items you rely on — bottles, bags, cups, cutlery.
  - Replace them with reusable alternatives.
  - Use them consistently for at least a week.
  - Note the single-use items you avoided.
  - Photograph your reusable items in use.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you swap? Was it easy to stick to? What did you enjoy?

## 12.4 — Repair or repurpose instead of discarding
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the repaired/repurposed item
- **How to do this:**
  - Find something you'd normally throw out.
  - Decide whether to repair it or repurpose it into something new.
  - Do the work.
  - Put it back to use.
  - Photograph the result.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you save from the bin? How? What did you enjoy?

## 12.5 — Set up proper recycling sorting at home or school
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the sorting setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up clearly labelled bins for different recyclables.
  - Place them where they'll actually be used.
  - Show your household or class how to sort correctly.
  - Check it's being used properly.
  - Photograph your sorting setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** What does your system sort? Is it being used? What did you enjoy?

## 12.6 — Do a no-new-purchases challenge and log it
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the challenge log *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a category — clothes, gadgets, non-essentials — and a time period.
  - Commit to buying nothing new in it; borrow, repair, or do without.
  - Log the moments you would have bought something.
  - Complete the full period.
  - Photograph your challenge log.
- **Reflection prompts:** What was hardest to resist? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?

## 12.7 — Make a poster on responsible consumption
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research consumption and waste facts relevant to the UAE.
  - Pick a few clear, motivating points.
  - Design a poster.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which fact hit hardest? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 12.8 — Cook with minimal packaging and waste
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the meal/ingredients
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a meal using fresh, loose, or minimally packaged ingredients.
  - Shop with reusable bags and containers.
  - Cook it, using up what you buy.
  - Compost the scraps.
  - Photograph your meal or its low-waste ingredients.
- **Reflection prompts:** How much packaging did you avoid? What did you enjoy?

## 12.9 — Organise a swap or second-hand event
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the event/setup
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan an event where people exchange clothes, books, or items instead of buying new.
  - Get permission for a space.
  - Spread the word and set simple rules.
  - Run it; donate leftovers.
  - Photograph the event setup.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you organise? How much was kept in use? What did you enjoy?

## 12.10 — Make reusable items from waste materials
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the reusable item
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather clean waste materials — fabric, jars, containers, packaging.
  - Make something reusable from them — bags, storage, organisers.
  - Make sure it's durable enough to actually use.
  - Put it to use.
  - Photograph your reusable item.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you make? Will it last? What did you enjoy?
