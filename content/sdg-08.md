# SDG 8 — Decent Work and Economic Growth

*Note: SDG 8 is a soft SDG — few photographable individual actions. Mostly "create / document" pattern.*

## 8.1 — Learn a job skill and build a portfolio piece
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the portfolio piece *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a real, marketable skill — design, coding, writing, a craft, video editing.
  - Work through a proper tutorial or course.
  - Create one finished piece that shows the skill.
  - Save it as the start of a portfolio.
  - Photograph your finished portfolio piece.
- **Reflection prompts:** What skill did you build? How could it lead to work? What did you enjoy?

## 8.2 — Start a small ethical micro-venture and document it
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the product/service *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Think of a small, honest way to create value — a handmade product, a service, baked goods.
  - Make sure it's fair: fair pricing, honest claims, no harm.
  - Produce your first product or deliver your first service.
  - Keep simple records.
  - Photograph your product or service.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you create? Was it fair and honest? What did you enjoy?

## 8.3 — Interview someone about their career path
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written interview/summary *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose someone whose work interests you.
  - Prepare questions about how they got there, what they do, what they'd advise.
  - Listen and take notes.
  - Write up what you learned.
  - Photograph your written summary.
- **Reflection prompts:** What surprised you about their path? What advice stuck? What did you enjoy?

## 8.4 — Create a CV or skills profile
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the CV/profile *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - List your skills, experiences, and strengths honestly.
  - Lay them out clearly in a CV or skills profile.
  - Keep it accurate — no inflation.
  - Ask someone for feedback and refine it.
  - Photograph your finished CV or profile.
- **Reflection prompts:** What strengths did you identify? What will you build next? What did you enjoy?

## 8.5 — Make a guide on workers' rights in the UAE
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research basic labour rights in the UAE — contracts, working hours, rest, fair treatment.
  - Pick the key points everyone should know.
  - Design a clear guide.
  - Share it where it could be useful.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** What right is least known? Where did you share it? What did you enjoy?

## 8.6 — Volunteer your skills for a community project
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the volunteering activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a skill you can offer — tech help, design, tutoring, building, organising.
  - Find a community project or group that needs it.
  - Commit to a real piece of work, not a token gesture.
  - Deliver it.
  - Photograph yourself doing the volunteer work.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you contribute? Who benefited? What did you enjoy?

## 8.7 — Practice and document a practical trade skill
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the work produced *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a hands-on trade skill — basic carpentry, sewing, electronics repair, gardening.
  - Learn the fundamentals safely.
  - Complete a small real project with it.
  - Note how this skill has economic value.
  - Photograph the work you produced.
- **Reflection prompts:** What skill did you practise? Would you keep developing it? What did you enjoy?

## 8.8 — Create a budget or business plan for an idea
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the written plan *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Take a venture idea — yours or hypothetical.
  - Work out realistic costs, pricing, and how it would be sustainable.
  - Write it up as a simple plan.
  - Get feedback from someone with experience.
  - Photograph your written plan.
- **Reflection prompts:** Is the idea viable? What did the numbers teach you? What did you enjoy?

## 8.9 — Organise a skills-sharing session
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the session
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather a group where each person teaches one useful skill.
  - Arrange a space and time.
  - Structure it so everyone both teaches and learns.
  - Run the session.
  - Photograph the skills-sharing session.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you teach and learn? Did it work well? What did you enjoy?

## 8.10 — Map career paths in a UAE growth industry
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written map/research *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a growing UAE sector — renewable energy, tourism, tech, logistics, space.
  - Research the range of jobs in it and what they need.
  - Lay it out as a clear career map.
  - Share it with classmates thinking about their future.
  - Photograph your finished map.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which path appeals to you? What surprised you? What did you enjoy?
