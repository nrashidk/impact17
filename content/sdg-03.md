# SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-being

## 3.1 — Complete a 30-day movement streak and log it
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the activity log/tracker *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a daily movement you can sustain — a walk, a sport, a workout.
  - Track each day on a calendar, app, or written log.
  - Keep it realistic so you can actually maintain it.
  - Complete the full streak.
  - Photograph your completed log.
- **Reflection prompts:** What movement did you choose? How did you feel by the end? What did you enjoy?

## 3.2 — Cook a balanced, healthy meal
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the meal
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan a meal with vegetables, a protein, and a whole grain.
  - Cook it yourself rather than buying it ready-made.
  - Keep added sugar and salt moderate.
  - Serve it to yourself or family.
  - Photograph your finished meal.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you make? How is it balanced? What did you enjoy?

## 3.3 — Organise a group walk, sport, or fitness session
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the group activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick an activity and invite family, friends, or classmates.
  - Choose a safe place and a cooler time of day.
  - Make it inclusive so everyone can join in.
  - Run the session.
  - Photograph the group activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you organise? Who joined? What did you enjoy?

## 3.4 — Create a mental well-being routine and document it
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the written routine *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Design a simple routine — short breaks, sleep schedule, time outdoors, journaling, time offline.
  - Write it down clearly.
  - Try it for several days.
  - Note what helped.
  - Photograph your written routine.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's in your routine? What helped most? What did you enjoy?

## 3.5 — Take a first aid or CPR awareness session and document it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of session notes or certificate *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a first aid or CPR awareness session — many UAE community centres and schools run them.
  - Attend and take notes on the key steps.
  - Summarise what you learned.
  - Keep any certificate or notes.
  - Photograph your notes or certificate.
- **Reflection prompts:** What's the most important thing you learned? When could it matter? What did you enjoy?

## 3.6 — Make a healthy-habits guide for younger students
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the guide *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a few habits — hydration, sleep, screen breaks, movement.
  - Write and illustrate a simple guide aimed at younger children.
  - Keep the language and visuals age-appropriate.
  - Share it with a younger class, sibling, or community group.
  - Photograph your finished guide.
- **Reflection prompts:** Which habits did you focus on? Who did you share it with? What did you enjoy?

## 3.7 — Prepare a week of healthy snacks
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the prepared snacks
- **How to do this:**
  - Plan simple, healthy snacks — cut fruit, nuts, yoghurt, vegetable sticks.
  - Prepare a week's worth and store them properly.
  - Replace one regular processed snack with these.
  - Note the difference over the week.
  - Photograph your prepared snacks.
- **Reflection prompts:** What snacks did you prepare? What did they replace? What did you enjoy?

## 3.8 — Do a digital detox session and document it
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo representing the offline activity *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a block of time to go screen-free.
  - Plan an offline activity — reading, sport, time outdoors, a hobby.
  - Put devices away fully for the chosen period.
  - Notice how you feel afterwards.
  - Photograph your offline activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** How long did you go offline? What did you do instead? What did you enjoy?

## 3.9 — Plant or tend a calming green space
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the green space
- **How to do this:**
  - Set up a small green corner — plants on a balcony, a windowsill garden, a tended patch.
  - Choose easy, climate-suitable plants.
  - Spend time tending it regularly.
  - Use it as a place to slow down.
  - Photograph your green space.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you plant? How does tending it feel? What did you enjoy?

## 3.10 — Organise a health awareness display
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the display
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a health topic relevant to your community — hydration in heat, sun safety, sleep, nutrition.
  - Research accurate information.
  - Build a display — posters, a stand, a presentation board.
  - Set it up where people will see it, at school or in your community.
  - Photograph your display.
- **Reflection prompts:** What topic did you choose? Where did you display it? What did you enjoy?
