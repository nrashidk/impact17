# SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure

## 9.1 — Build a working prototype that solves a small problem
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the prototype
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a small, real problem around you.
  - Sketch a solution and gather materials.
  - Build a working prototype — it doesn't need to be polished, it needs to work.
  - Test it against the problem.
  - Photograph your prototype.
- **Reflection prompts:** What problem does it solve? Does it work? What did you enjoy?

## 9.2 — Repair something instead of replacing it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the repaired item
- **How to do this:**
  - Find something broken — electronics, furniture, clothing, a tool.
  - Diagnose what's wrong.
  - Repair it safely rather than buying new.
  - Confirm it works again.
  - Photograph the repaired item.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you fix? What did repairing it save? What did you enjoy?

## 9.3 — Code a small app, tool, or website
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo/screenshot of the working build
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a simple, useful thing to build — a tool, a calculator, a small site.
  - Choose a language or platform and work through it.
  - Build until it actually runs.
  - Test it.
  - Screenshot or photograph your working build.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you build? What was hardest? What did you enjoy?

## 9.4 — Design a solution for a local infrastructure problem
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the design/plan *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Identify a real local issue — shade gaps, unsafe crossings, poor recycling access.
  - Research the problem properly.
  - Design a realistic solution — drawings, a model, a written proposal.
  - Get feedback.
  - Photograph your design.
- **Reflection prompts:** What problem did you tackle? Is your solution realistic? What did you enjoy?

## 9.5 — Make a model of sustainable infrastructure
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the model
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a sustainable infrastructure concept — a solar building, a green roof, efficient transport.
  - Build a physical or digital model of it.
  - Make the sustainable features clear.
  - Be ready to explain how it works.
  - Photograph your model.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you model? What makes it sustainable? What did you enjoy?

## 9.6 — Learn a digital skill and create something with it
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the output *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose a digital skill — 3D design, data visualisation, app prototyping, electronics.
  - Work through a proper tutorial.
  - Make something real with it.
  - Save the output.
  - Photograph what you created.
- **Reflection prompts:** What skill did you learn? What did you make? What did you enjoy?

## 9.7 — Upcycle materials into something useful
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the upcycled item
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather materials otherwise headed for waste.
  - Design something genuinely useful from them.
  - Build it.
  - Put it to use.
  - Photograph your upcycled item.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you make, from what? Is it actually useful? What did you enjoy?

## 9.8 — Make a poster on a UAE innovation or megaproject
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research a UAE innovation — the Mars mission, Masdar City, a smart-city initiative.
  - Pick a few facts that show the ambition and the impact.
  - Design a poster.
  - Display or share it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection prompts:** What project did you choose? What impressed you? What did you enjoy?

## 9.9 — Run a simple experiment and document the results
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the experiment/results *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Form a question you can actually test.
  - Design a fair, simple experiment.
  - Run it and record what happens.
  - Write up your results and conclusion.
  - Photograph your experiment or written results.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you test? What did you find? What did you enjoy?

## 9.10 — Teach someone a tech or making skill
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the teaching activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a tech or making skill you have.
  - Find someone who wants to learn it.
  - Break it into clear steps and guide them.
  - Let them build or try it themselves.
  - Photograph the teaching activity.
- **Reflection prompts:** What did you teach? How did they do? What did you enjoy?
