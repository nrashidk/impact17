# SDG 1 — No Poverty

*Note: SDG 1 has few naturally photographable individual actions. Most actions here are the "create an artefact / do a documented activity" pattern.*

## 1.1 — Assemble and donate a care package
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of assembled package
- **How to do this:**
  - Put together essentials: non-perishable food, hygiene items, or clean clothes in good condition.
  - Find a recipient channel — Emirates Red Crescent, a local mosque collection, or a community drive.
  - Pack items clearly and respectfully.
  - Deliver or drop off at the collection point.
  - Photograph your assembled care package before donating.
- **Reflection:** What did you include and why? Where did it go? What did you enjoy?

## 1.2 — Donate usable items to a charity collection
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of items at/near a donation point
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather clothes, books, toys, or household items in good condition.
  - Locate a UAE donation point — Emirates Red Crescent bins, Dubai Charity Association, or a school drive.
  - Clean and sort the items before donating.
  - Take them to the collection point.
  - Photograph your items at the donation point.
- **Reflection:** What did you donate? Why those items? What did you enjoy?

## 1.3 — Volunteer at a food bank or community kitchen
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the user volunteering
- **How to do this:**
  - Find a UAE food distribution effort — UAE Food Bank, Emirates Red Crescent, or a Ramadan iftar drive.
  - Register for a volunteer slot in advance.
  - Show up on time, follow the team's instructions, and commit for the full session.
  - Respect the dignity and privacy of everyone you serve.
  - Photograph yourself volunteering (with permission, no recipients identifiable).
- **Reflection:** What did you do during your session? What did you learn? What did you enjoy?

## 1.4 — Make an informational poster about a UAE social support service
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of the poster *(rewritten)*
- **How to do this:**
  - Research one real UAE support service — UAE Food Bank, Community Development Authority programmes, or Zakat Fund.
  - Note who it helps and how someone accesses it.
  - Design a clear poster — by hand or digitally.
  - Display or share it where people who need it might see it.
  - Photograph your finished poster.
- **Reflection:** Which service did you choose? What surprised you? What did you enjoy?

## 1.5 — Organise a small fundraising activity
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the fundraising activity
- **How to do this:**
  - Pick a registered UAE cause and a simple format — a bake sale, a sponsored walk, a class collection.
  - Get any needed permission from your school, building, or community.
  - Run the activity and keep the proceeds transparent.
  - Donate the total to the chosen cause through official channels.
  - Photograph your fundraising activity in progress.
- **Reflection:** What did you organise and for whom? What was challenging? What did you enjoy?

## 1.6 — Cook and share a meal with someone in need
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the prepared meal

## 1.7 — Write and share your own "budgeting basics" guide

# SDG 2 — Zero Hunger

## 2.1 — Grow a vegetable from seed to harvest
- **Effort:** Hard | **Points:** 20 | **Verification:** Photo of the growing/harvested vegetable
- **How to do this:**
  - Choose something that grows in the UAE climate — tomatoes, chillies, leafy greens, in cooler months.
  - Use a pot or garden bed with good soil and drainage.
  - Water consistently, morning or evening, and protect from peak afternoon sun.
  - Be patient — track it through to harvest.
  - Photograph your growing or harvested vegetable.
- **Reflection:** What did you grow? What was hardest? What did you enjoy?

## 2.2 — Donate non-perishable food to a UAE food drive
- **Effort:** Easy | **Points:** 5 | **Verification:** Photo of food items at/near a collection point
- **How to do this:**
  - Gather non-perishable items — rice, lentils, canned goods, dates — within date.
  - Find a collection point — UAE Food Bank, a mosque, or a supermarket drive.
  - Check expiry dates and pack securely.
  - Drop off at the collection point.
  - Photograph your donation at the collection point.
- **Reflection:** What did you donate? Why those items? What did you enjoy?

## 2.3 — Cook a meal using only leftovers or about-to-expire food
- **Effort:** Medium | **Points:** 10 | **Verification:** Photo of the prepared meal
- **How to do this:**
  - Check your fridge for food near its use-by date or leftovers needing using up.
  - Plan a meal around what you find rather than buying new ingredients.
  - Cook it and serve it.
  - Note how much food you saved from being wasted.
  - Photograph your finished meal.
- **Reflection:** What did you make? What would have been wasted otherwise? What did you enjoy?

  - Photograph your planted patch.
- **Reflection:** What did you plant? Who will help maintain it? What did you enjoy?

## 2.5 — Make a meal plan that reduces food waste

- **How to do this:**

  - Write or print the plan.
  - Photograph your finished meal plan.
